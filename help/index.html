Getting started

What is Studic?

Studic helps you focus on your studies. Use a simple timer, or a strict mode that keeps distracting apps closed until your study time is over.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed, and Studic works without internet. You only need to be online to watch an ad, buy or restore points, or read these Help pages.

How do I start a session?
  1. On Home, tap Start Focus.
  2. Pick Normal, Deep or Schedule.
  3. Choose how long, and for Deep, your apps. Then start.

The first time you use Deep or Schedule, Studic guides you through a quick one-time setup. Each permission is explained on the Permissions page.

Which phones does Studic work on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer. You can switch to a dark look with Settings → Dark Theme.

Focus modes

Normal mode
  • Free. It does not use points.
  • Does not block any apps.
  • You can tap Stop any time.
Deep mode
  • Strict. No early exit: the session ends when the timer ends.
  • Only your study apps (plus Phone and Messages) work. Other apps stay closed.
  • Uses points when it starts. See Points & Pro.
  • Needs the quick one-time setup.

How long: 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour, then every hour from 2 hours up to 12 hours.

Scheduled mode

Set a daily study time and Studic starts a strict session for you. It follows the same rules, points and breaks as Deep.

Open Modes → Schedule → Manage, then add a schedule with:

  • A name, start time and end time
  • The days of the week
  • Auto-start on or off

Each schedule can be up to 12 hours, and all your schedules together can add up to 12 hours. Schedules that overlap can’t be saved.

When a schedule starts and you are in another app, Studic brings you back to your timer. If Auto-lock during focus is on, it also locks the screen. If a Normal session is running, it stops and the strict session begins. If you do not have enough points at that moment, auto-start is switched off for that schedule.

During a strict session

Which apps can I use?
  • Study apps: open during focus time and during breaks.
  • Break apps: open only during a break.
  • Phone and Messages always work.
  • 2 study apps and 2 break apps are included free. Each extra app costs 3 points, shown before you confirm.
  • Your phone’s home screen stays closed during focus time and opens during a break.
  • Your phone’s Settings stay closed until the session ends.
What happens if I leave the session?

Studic brings your timer back.

If Settings → Auto-lock during focus is on (it is on by default), Studic also locks the phone, the same way the power button does. When you unlock with your usual PIN, pattern or biometrics, you are back on the timer. Studic cannot read your private information, erase your phone or change your password.

You can turn Auto-lock off any time in Settings. The timer still returns; the screen will not lock. Normal mode never locks the phone.

How do breaks work?

Longer sessions allow more breaks:

Up to 2 hours
No breaks
Over 2 up to 3 hours
1 break
Over 3 up to 4 hours
2 breaks
Over 4 up to 6 hours
3 breaks
Over 6 up to 8 hours
5 breaks
Over 8 up to 12 hours
6 breaks
  • Each break lasts 15 minutes and costs 3 points when it starts.
  • Resume Focus ends a break early. The points are not returned.
  • Your timer shows how many breaks you have left.
Can I stop a Deep or Scheduled session early?

No, and that is the point. There is no End button, so choose a length you can finish. Phone and Messages still work if you need to reach someone. If you are new, start with 15 or 30 minutes.

What if my phone restarts during a session?

Studic shows a short screen, brings your session back and may add some extra time to what is left. If a Scheduled session was already running when the phone started, it carries on without extra time.

Points are used when a session starts or a break begins. Finishing a session does not earn points.

How do I get points?
  • Daily top-up: at midnight, if you have 12 points or fewer, Studic tops you back up to 12 (Starter only).
  • Watch an ad: +15 points when the ad finishes (needs internet; hidden if you are Pro). Up to 5 successful ads per day, with a short wait between them.
  • Buy a pack in the store. Any pack makes you Pro while your balance stays at 12 or more.

Sharing Studic with a friend opens your phone’s share menu and does not add points. You can find Watch ad from the side menu, Home, session complete, or when you need more points.

What is Pro?
  • Buying any points pack makes you a Pro user.
  • No ads while you are Pro.
  • No free midnight top-up. You keep the points you have.
  • Session prices stay the same.

Pro stays active while you have 12 or more points. If you drop below 12, Pro pauses until you buy a pack or earn your points back up.

My purchase did not arrive
  1. Connect to the internet.
  2. Open Settings → Purchase History.
  3. Tap Restore purchases. Studic checks Google Play for any pack you paid for but did not receive.

“Not enough points”

Points saved for your auto-start schedules can’t be spent elsewhere. The message shows what you have, what is saved and what you need. Earn or buy more points, or choose fewer extra apps.

One-time setup

What is the setup for Deep and Scheduled?

A short guide the first time you use Deep or Scheduled. It helps you turn on:

  1. Usage access: so Studic knows which app is on screen during the session you started.
  2. Device admin: so Studic can lock the screen when you leave, if Auto-lock is on. Lock-screen only; it cannot wipe the phone or change your password.
  3. Display over other apps: a short recovery screen if the phone restarts mid-session. Not a daily overlay.
  4. Background running: so your phone does not stop Studic mid-session.
  5. Auto-start: only on phones that have this extra switch.

You can check them any time in Settings → Permissions Manager. Full wording for Play review is on the Permissions page.

Is my information safe?

Yes. During a strict session Studic only checks the name of the app on screen. It never reads your messages, passwords or anything you type, and nothing is sent to a Studic server. Device admin cannot erase your phone or change your password. Read more in our Privacy Policy and Permissions.

Can I turn a permission off?

Yes, in your phone’s Settings. Deep and Scheduled will stop working until you turn it back on. Normal mode only needs notifications.

Notifications

Where do I control Studic notifications?

Open Settings → Notifications Manager.

  • Session timer (on by default): remaining time while you focus. Turn off if you do not want that on the lock screen.
  • Hide system notifications (off until you allow Notification access): during a session, dismisses other apps’ shade notifications. Study apps stay; alarms and calls stay. Notification text is not uploaded.
  • Schedule skipped (on by default): a notice if a window cannot start because of points.

There is no Settings row for the quiet focus reminder. After you have completed sessions, Studic may ping at your usual start hour if you miss a day (daily habit) or on quiet day 3 and 4 otherwise. Opening Studic resets that streak. Studic does not send sales alerts.

Notifications are off

Allow notifications for Studic so you can see the timer, skip notice and reminder. If they stay off, Studic still works; scheduled sessions can still start. Use Notifications Manager or Android Settings to allow them again.

Backup & data

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Open Settings and use these two options:

  • Export Backup saves a backup file (settings, schedules, history and purchase records) wherever you choose.
  • Import backup restores from that file. Your settings and schedules are replaced, and your history is added to. You see a summary and confirm before anything changes.

Studic does not have its own online backup. If your phone’s own backup is on, Android may also bring your data back on a new phone.

Will importing give me back points I used?

No. Your points total is not copied from the file, and points you already used stay used. A pack you bought is added only if that phone has not saved the purchase yet.

Import is blocked

You can’t import or restore while a focus session is running, so finish the session first. Files that are damaged, changed, empty, too large or made by a newer version of Studic can’t be used.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your phone. Use Settings → Uninstall Studic, or clear Studic’s storage in your phone’s app settings.

Fixes

My session stopped or apps were not blocked

Some phones close apps in the background to save battery. Check Settings → Permissions Manager, then:

  • Let Studic run in the background (no battery restriction).
  • Turn on Auto-start for Studic if your phone has it (common on Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei and Samsung). The setup screen shows steps for your phone.
  • Make sure Usage access, Device admin and Display over other apps are on.
A schedule did not start
  • The one-time setup must be finished.
  • Auto-start must be on for that schedule.
  • You need enough free points when it starts.
  • Check background running and auto-start (above), and that your phone was switched on.
  • Allow alarms and reminders for Studic if your phone asks.
“No ad available” or “You’re offline”

Turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again. Sometimes no ad is ready, so try again a little later. You can review your ad choice any time with Ad privacy settings on the Watch ad screen.

How do I remove Studic?

Use Settings → Uninstall Studic. You can also uninstall it from your phone’s Settings once no strict session is running.
