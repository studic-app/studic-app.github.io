Modes

Choose how strict you want to be

Start light. Go deep when exams are close.

Normal

A focus timer you can stop any time.

  • Session timer and notification
  • Stop whenever you like
  • Free, uses no points
  • No app blocking

Deep

Your phone becomes a study device until time is up.

  • Only your study apps work, plus Phone and Messages

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Studic need an account or internet?

No account is needed, and Studic works without internet. Your sessions, schedules and history stay on your phone. You only need to be online to watch an ad or buy points.

Can I still use apps I need for studying?

Yes. In Deep and Scheduled modes you choose study apps for focus time and break apps for breaks. Phone and Messages always stay available.

Can I stop a Deep session early?

Deep and Scheduled sessions are strict on purpose, so there is no End button. If you want to be able to stop any time, use Normal mode.

How long can a session be?

From 15 minutes up to 12 hours. Sessions longer than 2 hours allow breaks, and longer sessions allow more of them.

How do points work?

Normal mode is free. Deep and Scheduled use points. You get a free daily top-up, and you can earn more by sharing Studic or watching an ad, or buy a points pack. Buying any pack makes you a Pro user and removes ads.

Why does Studic ask for extra permissions?

Normal mode needs almost nothing. Deep and Scheduled need a few extra permissions so Studic can keep other apps closed and lock your screen if you try to leave. Studic only asks when you set up those modes. See what each one does.
