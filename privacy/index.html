Who we are

Studic is a focus app for students, made by AmirSoftEng (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the Studic app and this website treat your information.

Studic works without an account, so we do not need to know who you are.

What Studic saves on your phone

To work properly, Studic remembers the following on your phone. It stays on your device and we cannot see it.

Your focus history
Which mode you used, when each session started and ended, how long it lasted, whether you finished it, and the breaks and points used.
Your points
Your points balance and a record of the points packs you bought.
Your schedules
The name, times, days and auto-start choice for each schedule you create.
Your settings and apps
Your theme, Auto-lock during focus, notification switches, and the apps you picked as study apps and break apps.
Your running session
The timer and break details of a session in progress, so Studic can pick it back up if your phone restarts.
When you last opened Studic
Used only on the phone to time a quiet focus reminder. Opening the app resets that quiet streak.
Studic does not keep a cloud list of the apps you open. During a Deep or Scheduled session it checks the name of the app on screen so allowed study apps stay available. If Usage access is granted, Home can also show an on-device app-usage chart; those totals are not uploaded.

Permissions

The full list, including Android permission names, what Studic never does, and how you turn each one off, is on the Permissions page. That page is the one to send in a Play review if a reviewer asks why a permission is in the APK.

Normal mode only needs notifications. Deep and Scheduled ask for extra permissions in a short, one-time setup. You can turn any of them off later in Android Settings or in Studic (Settings → Permissions Manager and Notifications Manager).

Notifications
Session timer, schedule-skipped notice, and an occasional focus reminder. You can switch channels off in Notifications Manager. Studic does not send sales alerts.
Usage access
Lets Studic know which app is on screen during a Deep or Scheduled session you started, so your study apps stay open and other apps stay closed. It only uses the app’s name. Nothing is sent to a Studic server. After you grant this, Home can also show an on-device app-usage chart.
Device admin
Used only to lock your screen when you leave a Deep or Scheduled session. Studic cannot read your private information, erase your phone or change your password. Locking happens only if Settings → Auto-lock during focus is on (it is on by default; you can turn it off). The only admin policy is lock-screen. Uninstall remains possible.
Display over other apps
A short recovery screen if the phone restarts during a Deep or Scheduled session. Not a daily overlay and never used for ads.
Background running
Keeps a session you started from being stopped by battery savers, and helps schedules start on time.
Notification access (optional)
Off until you turn on Hide system notifications. During a session, other apps’ shade notifications can be dismissed. Notification text is not read for ads and is not uploaded. Study apps, alarms and calls are left alone.
Studic does not use Accessibility, Device Owner, kiosk mode or screen pinning. During a Deep or Scheduled session, Android Settings may stay closed so the session cannot be switched off by accident. You get full control back when the session ends, and you can uninstall from Settings → Uninstall Studic.

Ads

Studic shows an ad only when you tap Watch ad to earn points. There are no ads during a running session. Pro users do not see ads. There is a daily cap (5 successful ads) and a short wait between paid ads.

Ads are provided by Google. To show an ad, Google may use information about your device and how you interact with the ad. That information goes to Google, not to us.

  • Where the law requires it, Studic asks for your choice first. You can review it any time from Ad privacy settings on the Watch ad screen.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone’s Settings under Privacy → Ads.

Learn more in How Google uses data in ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Points packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment. Studic never sees your card details or Google account.

After you buy, Studic keeps a record of the purchase on your phone so your points are added once, your purchase history can be shown, and your purchase can be restored if needed.

Payments and refunds follow Google Play’s own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing Studic

When you tap Share, your phone’s normal share menu opens with a short message and the Play Store link. You choose the app and the person. Studic does not know who you shared with and does not add points for sharing.

Backups

Your backup file

In Settings, Export Backup saves a backup file to a place you choose, and Import backup restores it later, for example on a new phone. The file includes your settings, schedules, history and purchase records. It is not locked with a password, so keep it somewhere private. Studic does not upload it anywhere and has no online backup of its own.

Your phone’s own backup

Android’s normal app backup works with Studic. If your phone’s backup is turned on, Android may save a copy of Studic’s information to your Google account and bring it back on a new phone. That copy is kept by Google, not by us, and you can manage or delete it in your phone’s backup settings.

What we don’t collect

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account details
  • Your messages, emails or passwords
  • Anything you type or view inside other apps, or a cloud copy of your browsing history
  • The text of other apps’ notifications
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files (except a backup file you choose to save or open)
  • Your exact location

We do not sell your personal information.

This website

You do not need to sign in or share any details to read these pages. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep basic visit records, such as your IP address.

If you choose light or dark mode in a browser, that choice is remembered on your device. Inside Studic, the pages simply follow the app’s look.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives on your phone, so you decide how long it stays. Uninstalling Studic (Settings → Uninstall Studic, or through Android) removes it. You can also clear Studic’s storage in your phone’s app settings.

Copies outside the app are separate: a backup file you saved, your phone’s cloud backup, and information Google holds for ads and purchases. You can manage those yourself or in your Google account.

Children

Studic is a study tool, but it is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are young, please ask a parent or guardian before watching ads or buying points.

Your choices

Because your Studic data is on your phone, you can see it in the app, save a copy with the backup feature, and remove it by uninstalling. For information held by Google, use Google’s privacy tools. If you have any questions, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Studic handles your information, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. For important changes, we will also let you know in the app.
