Focus modes

Normal

A free timer. It does not block any apps, and you can stop whenever you like.

Deep and Scheduled

Strict sessions that help you stay on task. While one is running:

  • There is no End button. The session finishes when the timer or schedule ends, up to 12 hours.
  • Only the study apps you chose (plus essentials like Phone and Messages) are available. Break apps open only during breaks.
  • If you leave, Studic brings you back and, if Auto-lock is on, locks your screen. Your phone’s Settings stay closed until the session ends.
  • If your phone restarts during a strict session, Studic gets you back to it and may add some extra time to what is left.

By starting a Deep session, or turning on auto-start for a Scheduled session, you ask Studic to apply these limits.

Some phone makers limit how apps can run. We work hard to keep Studic reliable, but we cannot promise it will work perfectly on every phone.

Using Studic safely

  • Do not start a strict session if you might urgently need other apps, for example for work, health or travel. If you are new, start with a short session.
  • Do not use Studic while driving, or anywhere that limiting your apps could put you at risk.
  • Check that your Scheduled windows suit your day, because they can start on their own.
  • Studic is a study helper. It does not promise grades, results or any health benefit.

Points and Pro

Points are used inside Studic only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged or transferred.

  • Normal sessions are free.
  • Deep and Scheduled sessions use points when they start. Extra study or break apps, and each break, also use points. Points that are used are not returned.
  • If your balance is low, free daily points may top it up. Watching ads, sharing Studic and buying points packs can add more.
  • Pro is unlocked when you buy any points pack. While you keep enough points, Pro removes ads and the free daily top-up. If your points drop too low, Pro pauses until you buy a pack or earn more points.
  • Points you buy do not expire.

Prices, rewards and offers (including limited-time bonuses) can change. The amounts shown in the app when you act are the ones that apply.

Purchases and refunds

  • Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy. Please ask Google Play for a refund, as we may not be able to refund a purchase ourselves.
  • If a purchase did not arrive, open Settings → Purchase History → Restore purchases.
